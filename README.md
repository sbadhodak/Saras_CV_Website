User Persona

My website is mainly designed for recruiters and companies looking for students for internships or junior positions, especially in the technology sector.
These users want to quickly find information about my education, programming skills, work experience and personal qualities.They are also interested in students who are motivated to learn and gain experience in real projects.
They usually don't have much time to review every CV, so they need a website that is clear, simple and easy to navigate. They also need to find my contact information easily if they are interested in my profile.


Information Architecture

For the information architecture, I decided to create a single-page website because I think it is easier for the user to find all the information in one place. I also wanted to keep the website simple because it is my first project of this type.
At the top of the website, I added a navigation bar with links to the different sections. This allows the user to go directly to the information they want to see.
The first section is my personal introduction. I included my name, a short description about myself and a picture. I also added a button to visit my GitHub profile and another one to contact me.
After that, I created an About Me section where I explain my interests and personal qualities. I wanted to show that I am interested in data engineering and cloud technologies, but also that I enjoy learning and working with other people.
The next section is Skills & Tools, where I decided to use the logos of the programming languages and technologies I have worked with during my studies. I think using logos makes the information easier to understand than writing a long list.
Then, I added my Work Experience. Although my current job is not related to Computer Engineering, I wanted to include it because it has helped me develop important skills.
Finally, I added my Academic Education, Languages and Contact information.
I think this order makes sense because the user first learns who I am, then what I know, what experience I have and finally how to contact me.

Visual Design

For the visual design, I wanted to create a website that looks professional but also a little different from a traditional CV.
Before starting, I looked at different personal websites for inspiration. One of the websites I liked the most was Kshitij's Portfolio (https://kshitijcodes.in/?ref=onepagelove), because of its simple design, typography and distribution of the information.
I decided to use a colour palette where beige is the main colour. I chose beige because I think it gives the website a clean and calm appearance. I combined it with dark burdeos, brown, light blue and olive colours to make some elements stand out.
For the typography, I used Playfair Display for the main titles because I wanted them to look more elegant. For the rest of the text, I used Montserrat, as I think it's simple and easy to read.
I also used rounded rectangles for the buttons, technology cards and other elements. For the technical skills, I added SVG logos because they look better than normal images and do not lose quality when changing their size.
Another important decision was the distribution of the sections. I tried to combine text with images and leave enough space between elements, so the website does not look too full.
For the contact section, I decided to use a dark background to make it different from the rest of the page.
